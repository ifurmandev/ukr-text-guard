# Domain Context — human-corpus

## Glossary

- Another person — an author who is neither the plugin author nor an official body. NOT a second text by the plugin author or a second document of the same body.
- Author — one named person who wrote a text or, for an official document, the one body that adopted it. NOT the number of files (two laws adopted by the same body have one author).
- Basis for publication — the reason a text may be kept in the public repository: public domain, an official document outside copyright, or the plugin author's own text. NOT the proof that the text is human (the date of writing before 2023 is a separate register field).
- Candidate — a text the plugin author chose for the human set and recorded in the sources register before the analyzer was first run on it. NOT a sample (a sample is already a file in the check set).
- Covered genre — one of the four genres (business letters and mail; articles and blogs; technical and legal documents; fiction and informal style) that has at least one human sample of 150 words or more in the sources register. NOT a genre that is only planned.
- Selection rule — the fixed rule, set before the run, that gives the borders of a fragment (from the start of the work or section to the first paragraph end after 150 words). NOT a border picked after looking at the index.
- Sources register — the one file in the sample folder that records every candidate and, for every human sample, its author, genre, source, basis for publication, date of writing with a reference to its evidence, fragment borders and a log of every change made to the text, and gives the reason for every refused candidate. NOT the eval table (the published summary of results) and NOT the known-gap list.
